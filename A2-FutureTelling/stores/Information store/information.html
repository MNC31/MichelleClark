NOTES ON THE ATMOSPHERE

Why this place?

I opted for a nostalgic, retro, American Strip Mall style because I believe a portfolio is a work of my pasts. The idea of the whole website is to determine how I see the future, and I see my future as a gateway to the past.

As you explore, I want you to experience my memories, stories, and projects as a way to honor a past that no longer exists in the future. That is why the format of the mall is something rather older. Each represents a part of my childhood that I grew up with, despite how modern the projects inside them are.

A familiar place,
remembered a little differently.
